## Company Overview & Business Profile

ICICI Bank Limited is an Indian multinational bank and financial services company headquartered in Mumbai, with a registered office in Vadodara [Source 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICICI_Bank). It offers a wide range of banking products and financial services to corporate and retail customers through various delivery channels and its group companies [Source 2](https://www.icici.bank.in/about-us/about-us). These services include commercial banking, retail banking, project and corporate finance, working capital finance, insurance, venture capital, and private equity, investment banking, broking, and treasury products [Source 3](https://www.ibef.org/industry/banking-india/showcase/icici-bank). ICICI Bank was originally promoted in **1994** by ICICI Limited [Source 2](https://www.icici.bank.in/about-us/about-us). As of December 31, 2024, ICICI Bank had a network of **6,742 branches** and **16,277 ATMs & cash recycling machines** [Source 4](https://www.icici.bank.in/about-us/news-room/2025/performance-review-quarter-ended-december-31-2024). The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has identified ICICI Bank as a domestic systemically important bank (D-SIB) [Source 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICICI_Bank).

## Current Focus Areas & Future Strategy

ICICI Bank is currently investing in strengthening its technology infrastructure, enhancing digital channels, and simplifying processes to improve customer experience [Source 1](https://www.icicibank.com/ms/simplify/message-from-the-chairman.html). The bank is focused on expanding its reach in micromarkets and ecosystems, with a customer-centric approach [Source 2](https://www.icici.bank.in/ms/aboutus/annual-reports/2024-25/html/our-business-strategy.html).

Key initiatives include:
*   Enhancing digital platforms and core applications to handle higher business volumes [Source 1](https://www.icicibank.com/ms/simplify/message-from-the-chairman.html).
*   Deepening presence and harnessing business opportunities across ecosystems and micromarkets [Source 2](https://www.icici.bank.in/ms/aboutus/annual-reports/2024-25/html/our-business-strategy.html).
*   Leveraging data analytics and market intelligence to develop localized strategies and tailored propositions [Source 2](https://www.icici.bank.in/ms/aboutus/annual-reports/2024-25/html/our-business-strategy.html).
*   Expanding the use of FASTag for payments beyond highways to parking facilities [Source 2](https://www.icici.bank.in/ms/aboutus/annual-reports/2024-25/html/our-business-strategy.html).
*   Fostering collaboration with startups to co-create innovative solutions [Source 2](https://www.icici.bank.in/ms/aboutus/annual-reports/2024-25/html/our-business-strategy.html).

According to the Chairman, the bank is taking initiatives towards simplifying the banking experience for its customers, with the principle of ‘Fair to Customer, Fair to Bank’ underpinning their strategy [Source 1](https://www.icicibank.com/ms/simplify/message-from-the-chairman.html). The bank has set a target of becoming carbon neutral in Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions by fiscal 2032 [Source 1](https://www.icicibank.com/ms/simplify/message-from-the-chairman.html).
