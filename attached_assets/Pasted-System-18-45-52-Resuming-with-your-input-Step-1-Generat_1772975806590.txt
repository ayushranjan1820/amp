## Business Requirements Document: New Success Page Post-KYC

### 1. Introduction

This document outlines the functional requirements for the development of a new success page to be displayed to users immediately after they successfully complete the Know Your Customer (KYC) verification process. The purpose of this page is to provide clear confirmation of successful KYC completion, guide users on the next steps, and enhance the overall user experience.

### 2. Functional Requirements

#### 2.1. Display of Success Confirmation

*   **FR-001:** The system SHALL display a clear and prominent message confirming the successful completion of the KYC process.
*   **FR-002:** The system SHALL display the user's name (or a unique identifier) on the success page to personalize the confirmation.

#### 2.2. Next Steps Guidance

*   **FR-003:** The system SHALL provide clear and concise instructions on the immediate next steps the user can take (e.g., "Proceed to Dashboard," "Start Trading," "Explore Features").
*   **FR-004:** The system SHALL include a prominent call-to-action (CTA) button that navigates the user to their primary next destination within the application (e.g., Dashboard, Home Page).

#### 2.3. Information Display (Optional/Conditional)

*   **FR-005:** The system MAY display a summary of key account information relevant to the user's post-KYC status (e.g., account type, verification level).
*   **FR-006:** The system MAY display links to relevant support resources or FAQs related to account activation or initial usage.

#### 2.4. Error Handling and Redirection

*   **FR-007:** In the event of a system error or an issue preventing the display of the success page, the system SHALL redirect the user to a generic error page or back to the KYC status page with an appropriate error message.
*   **FR-008:** The system SHALL prevent direct access to the success page without prior successful completion of the KYC process. If a user attempts to access the page directly, they SHALL be redirected to the KYC process or a relevant login/status page.

#### 2.5. User Interface and Experience

*   **FR-009:** The success page SHALL be visually consistent with the overall application's branding and design guidelines.
*   **FR-010:** The page SHALL be responsive and display correctly across various devices and screen sizes (desktop, tablet, mobile).
*   **FR-011:** The page SHALL load quickly to ensure a seamless user experience.

#### 2.6. Analytics and Tracking

*   **FR-012:** The system SHALL track the successful display of the KYC success page for analytics purposes (e.g., number of users completing KYC, page views).
*   **FR-013:** The system SHALL track clicks on the "next steps" CTA button to monitor user flow.

#### 2.7. Security

*   **FR-014:** The success page SHALL not display any sensitive user information beyond what is necessary for confirmation and personalization (e.g., full account numbers, passwords).
*   **FR-015:** Access to the success page SHALL be authenticated.
