II. Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2023:

This Master Direction provides a comprehensive framework for the regulation of NBFCs based on a scale-based approach. While not the "latest" in the context of February 2026, it is a crucial document for understanding NBFC regulations.

📅 Publish Date: October 19, 2023 (Updated as on March 21, 2024 and November 10, 2023)
Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2023
Key Points from the Master Direction:

Scale-Based Regulatory Structure: The regulatory structure for NBFCs comprises four layers: Base Layer (NBFCs-BL), Middle Layer (NBFCs-ML), Upper Layer (NBFCs-UL), and Top Layer (NBFCs-TL) (Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2023).
Base Layer Composition: The Base Layer includes non-deposit taking NBFCs with an asset size below ₹1,000 crore and NBFCs undertaking specific activities like Peer-to-Peer lending (Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2023).
Middle Layer Inclusion: NBFC-D, CIC, NBFC-IFC, and HFC will be included in the Middle Layer or Upper Layer, as applicable. SPD and IDF-NBFC will always remain in the Middle Layer (Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2023).
Upper Layer Identification: The Upper Layer will consist of NBFCs specifically identified by the Reserve Bank as warranting enhanced regulatory requirements based on a scoring methodology (Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2023).
Applicability: The provisions of these Directions apply to various categories of NBFCs registered with the Reserve Bank, including NBFC-D, NBFC-ICC, NBFC-Factor, NBFC-MFI, NBFC-IFC, and IDF-NBFC (Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2023).
Regulations for Multiple NBFCs in a Group: The total assets of all NBFCs in a Group shall be consolidated to determine the threshold for their classification in the Middle Layer (Master Direction – Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Company – Scale Based Regulation) Directions, 2023).
