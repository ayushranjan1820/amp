“Meeting Preparation & Talking-Points Generator”
Agent 1 — User Clarification Agent

This agent interacts with the user to collect missing details for the meeting.

Key Tasks

Ask follow-up questions if the user hasn’t provided enough context

Validate important criteria before proceeding

Questions It Asks

Who are you meeting? (Name, Role, Company)

What is the purpose of the meeting?

What industry/domain is this? (Banking, Tech, Healthcare, etc.)

What outcome do you want? (Sales, Partnership, Strategy, Info gathering)

What format? (In-person, call, pitch deck needed, etc.)

Meeting date/urgency?

Sample interaction
User: “I am meeting the CFO of Bandhan Bank to discuss rural banking.”
Agent asks:

Which specific objectives do you have regarding rural banking?

What’s your value proposition?

Do you want data-driven insights, trends, competitive benchmarking, or recommendations?

🔹 Agent 3 — Talking-Points Builder Agent

This agent synthesizes all information into contextual, strategic talking points.

Talking Point Categories
🚀 1) Intelligent Warm Opening

• “I saw that Bandhan Bank has been focusing on expanding deposits in tier-2 & rural markets — that aligns with my understanding of rural banking growth drivers.”
• “I noticed your recent interview on financial inclusion; I’d love to hear more about your POV.”

📊 2) Data-Backed Insights

• “According to the latest RBI data, rural deposits have grown X% YOY — how do you see Bandhan’s positioning relative to peers?”
• “I noticed Bandhan has a 15% share in micro-loans in rural segments — is that an area you’re planning to aggressively scale?”

🧠 3) Strategy & Growth Opportunities

• “With the shift toward mobile banking, what’s your strategy for digital outreach in rural areas?”
• “Have you considered partnerships with fintechs to expand micro-lending responsibly?”

💡 4) Sales-Oriented Value Add

• “Given your push in rural banking, a data-analytics platform for customer segmentation could help optimize loan portfolios — would you be open to a pilot?”
• “Our engagements with other banks show X% cost savings — can I show you how that might apply?”

📌 5) Risk & Operational Challenges

• “What’s been your biggest constraint in achieving scale in remote regions — distribution costs? credit risk? tech adoption?”
• “How do you measure ROI for rural product deployments?”

🌱 6) Future-Focused Innovation Points

• “With AI/ML being key to financial services, how do you view its role in customer risk profiling for rural clients?”
• “Any initiatives planned around digital-first rural banking offerings for 2026?”
