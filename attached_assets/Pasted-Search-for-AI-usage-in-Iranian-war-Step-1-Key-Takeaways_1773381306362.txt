The US military leveraged advanced AI systems to enhance its operational capabilities:

This allowed for the rapid identification of Iranian targets, significantly shortening the "kill chain" from days to minutes. AI was used to detect changes like new construction, vehicle movements, or activity at sensitive Iranian sites by scanning extensive satellite and drone data [YouTube: US Military AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ut9Vr0KSa9E), [YouTube: AI in Warfare](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K5fy9-cl08s).
