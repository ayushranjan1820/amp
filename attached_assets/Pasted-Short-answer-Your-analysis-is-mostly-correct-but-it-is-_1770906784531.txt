2️⃣ Default Loss Guarantee (DLG) Guidelines — 8 June 2023 (Major Update)

👉 This is the next major circular after 2022.

Fintech risk-sharing arrangements with lenders

DLG capped at 5% of loan portfolio

Only allowed via cash/FD/bank guarantee

Proper provisioning & asset classification

Mandatory disclosure of DLG arrangements

Enhanced due diligence by banks/NBFCs

👉 RBI concern: fintechs taking credit risk without regulation.
